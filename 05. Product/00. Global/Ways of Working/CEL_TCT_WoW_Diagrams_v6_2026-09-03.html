Ways of working, the three flows

CEL, YCH Transport Control Tower, SPVB Vietnam. Companion to the ways of working guide, version 6.

1. Where things live

Pods work in the sub-module folders. Everything shared sits above them.

2. Mockup workflow

Four steps, three of them Pierre's. The pod builds inside a copy of the real mockup, never on a blank file.

3. Specs workflow

The same three steps for a specification, a register or a screen inventory. The pod releases what Pierre has approved.

The documents, and who owns them

Every document we keep, in plain English. If something is not on this list, it does not have a home yet.
